# ProjectBridge 🚀
### Turn Ideas into Impact

**ProjectBridge** is an AI-powered institutional resource-sharing platform designed to bridge the gap between students' project ideas and the resources available within educational institutions.

Students often struggle to identify, locate, and access the equipment, laboratories, tools, and facilities required to build their projects. Meanwhile, institutions have underutilized resources that could be made accessible to more students.

ProjectBridge addresses this challenge by making institutional resources easier to discover, access, manage, and share.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Educational institutions possess valuable equipment, laboratories, technical tools, and shared spaces. However:

- Students often don't know which resources are available.
- Finding the right equipment for a project can be difficult.
- Resource booking and approval processes can be inefficient.
- Institutional resources may remain underutilized.
- There is limited visibility into resource availability and usage.

## ✨ Our Solution

ProjectBridge provides a centralized platform where students can discover institutional resources, generate project-specific equipment requirements using AI, match those requirements against available inventory, and request access through structured booking and approval workflows.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. AI Project Resource Engine
- Enter a project idea in natural language.
- Generate a structured list of required equipment and components.
- Identify essential and optional resources.
- Get quantities, specifications, purposes, and alternatives.
- Discover the resources needed to turn an idea into a practical project.

### 2. Intelligent Resource Matching
- Matches AI-generated requirements against institutional inventory.
- Identifies available and unavailable resources.
- Highlights resources requiring approval.
- Distinguishes partial matches and unverified availability.
- Helps students understand what they can access before starting a project.

### 3. Direct Resource Finder
- Search for equipment, laboratories, and shared facilities.
- Explore resources without first creating a project.
- View resource information and availability.
- Discover relevant institutional facilities.

### 4. Resource Booking & Approval
- Submit resource booking requests.
- Track booking and approval status.
- Route requests to the appropriate resource manager.
- Support approval, rejection, and waiting-list workflows.

### 5. Check-in & Check-out Management
- Track resource collection and returns.
- Record initial and final resource conditions.
- Maintain booking and usage history.
- Support incident and dispute reporting.

### 6. Institutional Resource Management
- Organize resources by departments and laboratories.
- Manage equipment information and availability.
- Configure resource access requirements.
- Monitor bookings and resource utilization.

### 7. Role-Based Dashboards
Different interfaces support the needs of students, resource managers, faculty/lab managers, and institutional administrators.

---

## 🔄 How ProjectBridge Works

1. **Describe:** A student enters a project idea.
2. **Generate:** The AI creates a structured equipment and resource checklist.
3. **Match:** ProjectBridge compares the requirements with institutional inventory.
4. **Discover:** The student explores matched resources and their availability.
5. **Request:** The student submits a booking request.
6. **Approve:** The appropriate authority reviews the request.
7. **Access:** The student checks out the approved resource.
8. **Return:** The resource is returned and its condition is recorded.

## 🧪 Current Project Status

**Status: Functional Hackathon Prototype**

The current version demonstrates the major ProjectBridge workflows using a seeded demonstration database.

Implemented capabilities include:
- AI-based project resource planning.
- Inventory matching.
- Direct resource discovery.
- Booking and approval workflows.
- Check-in and check-out.
- Resource condition tracking.
- Role-based demonstration dashboards.

**Current prototype limitations:**
- Uses demonstration institution and resource data.
- New institution and user onboarding are not yet implemented.
- Production authentication and multi-institution deployment are future development goals.

The demonstration environment is intended for testing and hackathon evaluation, not for managing real institutional data.
